Privacy Policy — LUPA

Last updated: 22 November 2026

LUPA was previously called FoodScanner. Only the name has changed; this policy applies to the same app (Google Play package com.boris_k00.FoodScanner).

1. Overview

LUPA is an informational app that helps users identify food additives by scanning product barcodes, and that shows what a QR code contains. LUPA needs no account. We are committed to protecting your privacy.

2. Data We Collect

LUPA does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers. We have no servers. Only three things leave your device:

  • a scanned barcode number, to Open Food Facts (sections 4 and 5);
  • ad requests, to Google AdMob (section 5);
  • an error report if the app crashes or hits an error, to Sentry — only while “Send error reports” is on (section 6).

The app stores its own settings on your device (section 7).

3. Camera and Pictures

The app asks for camera access solely to scan barcodes and QR codes. No images or video are saved or transmitted.

If you choose a picture from your gallery, the barcode in it is read on your device. The picture is not uploaded or saved by the app.

4. Barcodes and QR Codes

When you scan a product barcode, or tap “Search product” on a QR code that holds a product code, the barcode number is sent to Open Food Facts to look up the product. The number identifies the product, not you.

The content of a QR code is read on your device and is not sent anywhere. A link in a QR code opens only if you choose to open it.

5. Third-Party Services

Open Food Facts: Product data is retrieved from the Open Food Facts public API (world.openfoodfacts.org). Only the barcode number is sent. No personal data is sent to this service.

Google AdMob: The app shows advertisements via Google AdMob and requests non-personalized ads only. AdMob may collect the advertising ID of your device. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and the UK), the app asks for your consent through Google's consent message before any ads are requested, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings › Privacy Policy. For more information, see Google's Privacy Policy.

Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.): receives error reports, see section 6.

6. Error Reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a short technical report to Sentry so that the problem can be found and fixed. Reports are stored in Sentry's EU data region (Frankfurt, Germany).

A report contains: the type of error and where in the app's code it happened; the app version; the Android version; the phone's maker and model and its technical state at that moment (for example free memory and storage, screen size, battery level, language and time zone); a random installation ID created by Sentry, which is not linked to you and is replaced when the app is reinstalled; and the technical steps just before the error (for example which screen was open, or that a product lookup failed with a given status).

A report never contains: anything you scanned (barcodes, product names, QR code content), pictures, your name or e-mail address, your advertising ID or your location. Before a report leaves your device, the app removes long numbers (such as barcodes and phone numbers), e-mail addresses and the details of web addresses from it. Sentry is set up not to store IP addresses.

Retention: Sentry deletes reports automatically after 90 days at the latest.

Your choice: error reports are on by default. You can turn them off at any time in Settings › Privacy Policy › Send error reports. While they are off, the app does not start Sentry at all and sends nothing. See also the Sentry Privacy Policy.

Legal basis (GDPR): our legitimate interest in keeping the app working reliably (Art. 6(1)(f)), with reports kept to the minimum above and the option to turn them off.

7. Data Stored on Your Device

The app stores only these settings, locally on your device:

  • your chosen language;
  • that you have accepted the terms of use, and when;
  • the scan category you chose last;
  • which launch animation comes next;
  • whether error reports are turned on.

This data never leaves your device. It is removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

8. Your Rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to access, correct and delete data about you and to object to its processing. Error reports cannot be linked to a person, so we usually cannot find a specific one; if you want a report deleted, write to us with the time it happened and your phone model and we will do our best. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority.

9. Children

This app is not directed at children under the age of 13.
